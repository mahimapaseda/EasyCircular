It is mandatory to consider the certificate awarded by this Ministry for trainees who have
successfully completed the ten-day teacher training programme as new teachers
implemented by the Seethawakapura Pirivena Teacher Training Institution as per the
approval given by the 202"4 Pirivena Education Board, when their service is confirmed at
the post and to be mentioned as such in the Letter of Appointment.
The Seethawakapura Pirivena Teacher Training Institution conducts a ten-day residential teacher
training workshop for new Parivenacharyas. At the end of this workshop, a certificate is awarded to
each of the Parivenacharyas who have participated continuously for ten days under this workshop.
