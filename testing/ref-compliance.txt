Author Compliance Guidelines (Strictly Enforced) 
 
1. Double-Blind Review Compliance 
• Submissions must be fully anonymized. 
• Author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, and identifying information must not 
appear in the manuscript. 
Violation will result in immediate desk rejection 
 
2. Paper Length Requirement 
• Papers must be a minimum of 4 pages and a maximum of 6 pages (inclusive of all 
content, references, and appendices). 
Papers not complying with page limits will be desk rejected 
 
3. Originality and Multiple Submission Policy 
• Submitting the same or substantially similar paper to: 
o Multiple venues, or 
o Multiple tracks within the same conference is strictly prohibited. 
Violations will result in immediate rejection of all instances of the same manuscript 
 
4. Authorship Declaration 
• All authors must be listed in the submission system at the time of submission. 
• The author list in the submission system must match exactly with the camera-ready 
submission if accepted . 
No additions, removals, or reordering of authors are allowed after the review process is 
completed. 
 
5. Plagiarism and Similarity Check 
• All submissions will be screened using Turnitin for plagiarism and AI content (Plagiarism 
<30% and AI content < 50%).

• Plagiarism, including self-plagiarism, is strictly prohibited. 
Papers exceeding acceptable similarity thresholds will be rejected without review 
 
6. Formatting Compliance 
• Papers must strictly follow official IEEE formatting guidelines and templates. 
Non-compliant submissions will be desk rejected. 
 
Failure to comply with any of the above requirements will result in Desk rejection without 
review
